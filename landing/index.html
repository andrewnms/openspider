The story

Meet Mofu. She styles wigs for cosplayers.

🎀
"I love the craft. I hate replying to the same DM 14 times a day."

Two years in. 200+ clients, 2,600+ messages a month. Same questions every day:

Inbox, every hour
"How much for Stelle styling po?"
Inbox, every day
"Slots open this month?"
Inbox, weekly
"Pwede po rush by Friday?"

She was burning out. She stopped posting. Lost momentum. Started turning away repeat customers because she couldn't keep up with new inquiries.

Then she trained kbrain on her business chats.

kbrain learned her pricing structure, the way she talks to clients, the platforms she uses (GCash, Lalamove), the workflow from inquiry → quote → deposit → styling → shipping. It started replying in her voice. She started posting again. Booked out by mid-month.

How it works

Four steps. One afternoon.

No SaaS signup. No vendor lock-in. kbrain runs as a single binary on your machine. The vault is plain markdown files.

1

Connect your data

Drop your chat exports (Facebook, WhatsApp, Telegram) into kbrain. It builds a structured map of clients, conversations, prices, deadlines.

2

Train your agents

kbrain analyzes your past replies and proposes agents to hire. You review them like a hiring manager — say yes, no, or edit the brief.

3

Plug in channels

Connect WhatsApp, Facebook, email. Agents listen on those channels and reply in your voice. You stay in the loop on edge cases only.

4

Operate by message

Talk to kbrain like an employee. "Take 4 commissions this month." "Stop replying to old leads." It does it. Reports back.

The agents you can hire

A team of specialists. Trained on your business.

Each agent is a small JavaScript program with full read/write access to your workspace. You can edit them, fork them, or hire new ones from the marketplace.

Stop replying to "magkano po?"

Pull your chat exports, install kbrain, hire your first agent in an afternoon. One Rust binary, one `bettersync` CLI, your existing WhatsApp.

Install kbrain
git clone github.com/kkodo/kbrain
cd kbrain && cargo install --path crates/kbrain-bin
brain init ~/my-vault
brain serve --vault ~/my-vault
Drive it with bettersync
npm i -g @kkodo/bettersync
bettersync auth login \
  --token kb_localdev \
  --endpoint http://127.0.0.1:7700/mcp
bettersync ingest ~/Downloads/fb-export
